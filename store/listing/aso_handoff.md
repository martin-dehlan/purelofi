# Screenshot handoff brief

Paste this into the design tool (Claude Design or similar) together with
the captures listed below. Headlines are proposals until Martin signs them
off; captures 2 to 5 still have to be taken on the phone.

## Context

PureLofi is a lo-fi music player for iPhone and Android. Every track comes
from a lo-fi musician, none of it is AI, and each one plays in a pixel-art
room that moves with the music. It is for people who keep lo-fi on while
they work, study, read or fall asleep. Target size: iPhone 6.9"
(1320 × 2868 px, portrait). Alternates: 6.7" 1290 × 2796, 6.5" 1242 × 2688.

## Set-wide visual rules

- Background: Ember `#C06A33`, solid. No gradients, glows, radial patterns
  or light effects.
- Headlines: heavy sans serif (Inter Black or SF Pro Display Black), white,
  uppercase, centred. Line 1 is the verb and the largest; line 2 is the
  descriptor, smaller but still bold.
- Keep text inside the middle 70% of the width, at least 15% padding on each
  side.
- Headline sits in the top 20 to 25% of the canvas with room above it.
- Device: photorealistic modern iPhone with Dynamic Island, centred, placed
  high so the bottom of the phone runs off the bottom of the canvas.
- Every screenshot uses the same font, sizes, frame and background, so the
  set reads as one series.
- The pixel art inside the phone must stay sharp: scale the captures only by
  whole numbers or not at all, never smooth them.

## Per-screenshot brief

```text
1. PLAY LO-FI
   IN LIVING PIXEL ROOMS
   Capture: store/listing/captures/01-player.png (simulator, 1320 × 2868) — rated Great
   Shows: the Autumn Bookshop full screen, record player, steaming mug,
   the cat asleep on the stool, controls hidden.
   Breakout: none. The room is the point; let it fill the phone.
   Secondary: none.
```

```text
2. HEAR REAL MUSICIANS
   NO AI, EVER
   Capture: still to take — open the menu, tap "Behind the scenes" on a
   track that has footage, screenshot while the clip plays.
   Breakout: the video panel from the Behind the scenes sheet, scaled up
   and running past both edges of the phone at the same height, soft shadow.
   Secondary: none.
```

```text
3. KEEP LISTENING
   WITH THE SCREEN OFF
   Capture: still to take — play a track, lock the iPhone, wake the screen
   so the lock screen shows the room cover and the playback controls.
   Breakout: the lock screen's now-playing card, scaled up past both edges
   of the phone, soft shadow.
   Secondary: none.
```

```text
4. PICK YOUR ROOM
   SEVEN TO CHOOSE FROM
   Capture: still to take — open the menu, tap "Change scene", screenshot
   the list with every room's cover visible.
   Breakout: none.
   Secondary: none.
```

```text
5. SAVE YOUR FAVOURITES
   AND PLAY OFFLINE
   Capture: still to take — heart four or five tracks first, then open the
   library on the Favourites pill so the list is full, not empty.
   Breakout: none.
   Secondary: a small heart icon near the top right of the phone, white.
```

Taking the captures on the iPhone: dark mode (the app is dark anyway), full
battery, no notifications on screen, and the clock at a plain time.

## Anti-patterns

No watermarks. No text other than the two headline lines. No App Store
chrome. No gradients or glows on the background. No busy decoration. No
text near the canvas edges. No rotated breakout panels. Never call the
music guitar or bass, never say who played it.

## Output dimensions

- 6.9": 1320 × 2868 px, five images, PNG.
- If the tool returns a slightly different aspect ratio, crop from the
  centre-top: trim both sides equally and keep the top edge so the headline
  stays where it is.

How to use this brief: attach the five captures in the same chat or canvas
where you paste it.
